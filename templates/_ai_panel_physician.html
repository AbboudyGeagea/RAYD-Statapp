How is this calculated?
Physician referral trends are calculated using linear regression on daily referral counts over the selected date range. The 90-day forecast projects this trend forward assuming a constant slope. Physicians with a consistently negative trend are flagged as Declining; those with a positive trend as Growing. Data source: referring physician field from PACS studies (etl_didb_studies).
