DICOM Viewer Link
After login the patient is sent to their own study through a signed link that expires

Must contain {accession}, {expires} and {signature}; {mrn} and {nonce} are optional

Shared with the PACS viewer only. Stored encrypted, never shown again after saving.

30 – 3600. The patient must open the link within this time.

What the PACS viewer must check

payload = accession|mrn|expires|nonce

signature = hex( HMAC-SHA256( secret, payload ) )

Open that one study without a login only if the signature matches and the current Unix time is before expires; refuse a nonce already used. No shared viewer account reaches the patient, and changing the accession breaks the signature.
