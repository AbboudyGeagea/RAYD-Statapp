What “not in reports” means

These messages were received and are stored safely — nothing has been lost. RAY7 judged something about them serious enough that it did not copy their contents into the reporting tables.

The practical effect: for the studies listed below, the reports are missing or out of date. A study may not appear at all, or may be stuck at an earlier stage than it really reached, so counts and turnaround times that include it will be wrong.

To fix one: open the message to see what RAY7 objected to, correct the cause — usually mapping an unknown status code, or a sender reusing a message ID — then re-run python app.py -m to replay the stored messages. Resolving a finding on its own only clears the flag; it does not put the data into the reports.
