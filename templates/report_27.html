Why the raw numbers look different

The two systems count different things. A CT of the abdomen and pelvis is one study in PACS but two procedure steps in the RIS. {% if ag.steps_per_study %} Here the RIS averages {{ ag.steps_per_study }} steps for every study. {% endif %} Counting raw RIS steps therefore makes PACS look like it is missing reports when it is not. Every figure on this card groups multi-part exams back into single studies first, so both sides are counting studies.

Hourly Load (Volume Trend)
Number of orders scheduled per hour of the day, aggregated across the full audit period. Peaks show the busiest scheduling windows — useful for staffing and machine planning.
Proc-ID Reconciliation
Ratio of orders where the RIS procedure code exactly matched the PACS procedure code (Match) vs. those that didn't (Mismatch). High mismatches may indicate data entry errors or mapping gaps.
Busiest Day & Time (Weekly Pattern)
Each cell is a weekday x time-block combination. Background shade shows total order volume for that slot across the whole audit period (darker = busier); the line inside each cell shows how that slot's daily volume moved day-by-day across the period — hover a cell for exact dates and counts.
Busiest Day of Month
Number of orders scheduled per calendar day-of-month (1st–31st), summed across every month in the audit period. Recurring spikes (e.g. around the 1st) often track billing/insurance cycles rather than clinical demand.
Demographics (Age & Gender)
Order count by patient age group (0–8, 9–18, 19–64, 65+), stacked by gender. Reveals which age and gender segments are driving demand in the selected period.
Order Status Mix
Orders by lifecycle stage. Each bar groups several RIS statuses — hover to see exactly which ones, so the figures can be reconciled against the RIS worklist. A large Cancelled bar may indicate workflow issues.
Modality Distribution (AE)
Number of linked PACS studies per storing AE title (imaging device). Shows which physical machines received the most studies in the period — cross-reference with Infrastructure tab for utilization context.
