Technician TAT by AE station — one bubble per AE title/technician station. X = average TAT (min), bubble size = study count. Y is the median TAT rather than a repeat of study count — median_tat is a real second metric already present in the data, and plotting it against the average surfaces distribution skew directly on the chart (bubbles well below the dashed avg=median line mean a handful of slow outliers are dragging the average up above a otherwise lower typical case), which a count-based Y axis wouldn't show. Color tier follows the same 0-30 / 30-60 / 60m+ minute buckets used by the (now-removed, moved to Report 35) Technician Efficiency card — tech_tat_cards carries no per-row expected/target duration to compare against, so this falls back to that same fixed threshold instead.
